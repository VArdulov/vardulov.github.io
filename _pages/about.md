Welcome to my personal site!
======

I am Victor. Presently I am a Senior AI Engineer with 
[Workday AI Research](https://www.workday.com/en-us/artificial-intelligence/ai-research.html) where I work on developing
research related to recursive self-improvement of agents, specifically by studying the trajectories agents take through
reasoning and tool-calling in an effort to make them more either more efficient or more robust (or both!). Before
joining the research group I was a founding technical member of 
[Workday's decision intelligence platform](https://www.workday.com/en-us/ai-agents/decision-intelligence.html) to 
support financial, human resource, and payroll analysis and planning. Prior to joining Workday, I was a Staff AI 
Research Scientist at [Mendel AI](https://mendel.ai) (which was acquired by
[Ensemble Health Partners](https://www.ensemblehp.com/rcm-intelligence/)) working on fusing Large Language Models with 
Symbolic AI for robust medical reasoning, mostly applied to helping medical centers get paid by medical insurers. 

I completed my PhD at the University of Southern California in May of 2022, where I conducted my research as a member of
the [Signal Analysis and Interpretation Lab (SAIL)](https://sail.usc.edu) under the advisement of [Shrikanth Narayanan](https://sail.usc.edu/people/shri.html). Following 
my PhD, I worked briefly at AWS on what would become [HealthScribe](https://aws.amazon.com/healthscribe/).

During my time at SAIL, my research studied dyadic interactions where one of the interlocutors acts as a guide. My work 
demonstrated the advantages of modeling behavioral interactions as control-affine dynamical systems, thus utilizing 
existing techniques to analyze and optimize control policies in human-human interactions. By treating embeddings of 
language, speech, and body posturing as observational data to fit dynamical system models to we are able to better 
describe the individuals' behavioral dynamics and in turn navigate the interaction towards a desired outcome. 
Our work was supported via applications in domains such as truth identification in child interviews, automated 
psychotherapist evaluation, and differentiating behavioral disorders in children under diagnostic settings.

My interests sprang from a desire to improve equity and access to mental and behavioral health resources. With
the correct models, data, and objectives, these approaches would allow us to design therapeutic support tools which 
enable clinicians to more readily adapt their strategies to specific clients and situations. Both improving patient 
outcomes and managing their therapist burnout.

While at USC, I had the pleasure of meeting [Neil Serebryany](https://www.forbes.com/sites/brucerogers/2023/09/14/neil-serebryany-creates-calypsoai-to-make-ai-more-secure/) and co-founding [CalypsoAI](https://calypsoai.com) as the 
first technical member of the team and serving as the Chief Science Officer for 4 years. As CSO, I developed 
machine-learning model evaluation standards, software, and tools - establishing and codifying best practices in 
machine-learning, and exploring various methods for incorporating measured model testing into a product. CalypsoAI went 
on to be [acquired by F5](https://www.f5.com/company/news/press-releases/f5-to-acquire-calypsoai-to-bring-advanced-ai-guardrails-to-large-enterprises)
